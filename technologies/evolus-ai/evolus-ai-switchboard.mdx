# Evolus AI Switchboard

The [Evolus AI Switchboard](https://evolus.ai/it/centralino-ai) is an AI phone assistant that answers calls day and night with a natural voice.

| General | |
|---------|--|
| **Developer** | Evolus AI |
| **Type** | AI voice agent for phone calls |
| **Availability** | Included from the Starter plan |
| **Website** | [evolus.ai/it/centralino-ai](https://evolus.ai/it/centralino-ai) |

---

## Core Features

- **Always on**: answers calls day and night.
- **Requests and appointments**: gives information, opens requests, and schedules appointments.
- **Human handoff**: from the Growth plan, passes calls to a colleague with context when needed.
- **Transcripts**: transcribes every call, available by email, in the portal, and in the app.
- **Disclosure**: presents itself as an AI at the start of the call.

---

## Use Cases

- **Small businesses**: cover the phone outside office hours.
- **Appointment booking**: let callers schedule without waiting for staff.
