# Evolus Digital Employees

[Evolus Digital Employees](https://evolus.ai/it/dipendenti-ai) work like colleagues with their own mailbox and access, handling real tasks inside a company's existing tools.

## Core Features

- **Own mailbox and access**: each digital employee has an email address and calendar.
- **Meetings**: attends meetings, takes notes, and sends the minutes.
- **Learns procedures**: follows your procedures and asks when unsure.

---

## Pricing

No plan includes seats. Digital employees are added as needed at €99 per month per seat, starting from the Growth plan. Source: [evolus.ai](https://evolus.ai).

## Use Cases

- **Inbox handling**: triage and answer routine email.
- **Meeting follow-up**: record meetings and circulate minutes.
