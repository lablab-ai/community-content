# SO-101

[SO-101](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100) is an open-source robotic arm designed by TheRobotStudio in collaboration with Hugging Face, and is the next-generation successor to the SO-100 arm. Every part of the project, from the mechanical design and 3D-printable files to the control code and pretrained models, is published openly, and the arm pairs directly with Hugging Face's LeRobot library for teleoperation, data collection, and imitation-learning research. It is the reference hardware used in Intel's Physical AI Studio robotics challenges.

## Core Features

- **6 degrees of freedom** — driven by STS3215 bus servos delivering up to 30kg.cm of torque, with a 12-bit magnetic encoder and UART communication.
- **Fully open hardware** — every mechanical part is 3D-printable from published files, with a public bill of materials.
- **Leader/follower teleoperation** — ships as a passive leader arm and powered follower arm, the standard LeRobot setup for recording demonstration data.
- **Improved build over SO-100** — simplified wiring and no gear-removal step required during assembly, with updated leader-arm motors.

## Ecosystem and Integrations

- Used as the robotics hardware for [Intel Physical AI Studio](/technologies/intel/intel-physical-ai-studio) VLA training and policy execution workflows.
- Trained policies deploy through [OpenVINO](/technologies/intel/openvino) on [Intel Core Ultra](/technologies/intel/intel-core-ultra) processors for low-latency control loops.
- Pairs with [Anomalib](/technologies/intel/anomalib) in defect-detection-and-response pipelines, where SO-101 performs the physical response after a visual anomaly is flagged.
