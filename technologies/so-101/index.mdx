# SO-101

SO-101 is an open-source robotic arm designed by TheRobotStudio in collaboration with Hugging Face, and is the next-generation successor to the SO-100 arm. Every part of the project, from the mechanical design and 3D-printable files to the control code and pretrained models, is published openly, and the arm is built to pair directly with Hugging Face's LeRobot library for teleoperation, data collection, and imitation-learning research.

## Core Products

### SO-101 Arm
A 6 degree-of-freedom robotic arm using STS3215 bus servos with up to 30kg.cm of torque, a 12-bit magnetic encoder, and UART communication, designed for low-cost embodied AI research.

### Leader/Follower Teleoperation Kit
SO-101 ships as a leader-follower pair, letting an operator control a passive leader arm by hand to record motion data that drives the powered follower arm, the standard data-collection setup for LeRobot imitation learning.

## Key Features

**Fully Open Hardware**
Every mechanical part is 3D-printable from published files, and the full bill of materials is public, keeping the total build cost low compared to commercial robotic arm kits.

**Improved Assembly Over SO-100**
SO-101 simplifies wiring and removes a gear-removal step required in the original SO-100 arm, and uses updated motors on the leader arm.

**Native LeRobot Integration**
The arm is designed around Hugging Face's LeRobot library, so recorded teleoperation data feeds directly into LeRobot's imitation-learning training pipeline without custom integration work.

## Use Cases

**Imitation Learning Research**
Record human teleoperation demonstrations on the leader arm and train Vision-Language-Action policies with LeRobot, then deploy the trained policy back onto the follower arm.

**Physical AI Prototyping**
Pair SO-101 with an edge inference stack, such as [Intel Physical AI Studio](/technologies/intel/intel-physical-ai-studio) and OpenVINO, to prototype autonomous manipulation tasks like defect detection and response.
