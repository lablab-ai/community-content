# Modalix MLSoC

[Modalix MLSoC](https://sima.ai/mlsoc-family/) is SiMa.ai's second-generation Machine Learning System-on-Chip, announced as a System-on-Module (SoM) on March 10, 2025. It extends the original MLSoC line to multi-modal transformers, Large Language Models (LLMs), Large Multimodal Models (LMMs), and generative AI, while staying compatible with traditional CNN workloads, all within an edge-appropriate power envelope.

## Core Features

- **Machine Learning Accelerator (MLA)** — supports BF16, INT8, and INT16 precision for flexible performance-per-watt tuning.
- **Application processor** — 8x Cortex-A65 CPU cores at 1.4 GHz alongside the MLA, running Linux for full application hosting.
- **Memory** — 32GB or 8GB of LPDDR5 at 6400 Mbps depending on configuration.
- **Multiple form factors** — available as a System-on-Module, a PCIe companion card, and through the Modalix Development Kit for rapid prototyping.
