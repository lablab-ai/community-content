# AssemblyAI

AssemblyAI is a speech AI company founded in 2017 by CEO Dylan Fox. The company builds APIs that turn audio and voice into structured data and conversational experiences, spanning pre-recorded and real-time transcription, fully managed voice agents, and audio intelligence add-ons like sentiment analysis and PII redaction. AssemblyAI has raised approximately $145M across three funding rounds, most recently a $50M Series C in December 2023 led by Accel.

## Core Products

### [Speech-to-Text](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-speech-to-text)
Transcribes pre-recorded audio and video across 99 languages using the Universal-3.5 Pro model, with speaker diarization, PII redaction, and native code-switching for audio that moves between languages.

### [Universal-Streaming](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-streaming-speech-to-text)
Real-time transcription over WebSocket for live captioning and voice agents, built on the Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime model with sub-second latency.

### [Voice Agent API](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-voice-agent-api)
A fully managed speech-to-speech pipeline combining transcription, LLM reasoning, text-to-speech, turn detection, and tool calling behind a single WebSocket connection.

### [Speech Understanding API](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-speech-understanding)
Layers structured intelligence on top of transcripts: sentiment analysis, entity detection, topic detection, summarization, translation, and speaker identification.

### [Guardrails API](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-guardrails)
Safety and compliance controls for audio and transcripts, including PII redaction, profanity filtering, and content moderation.

### [LLM Gateway](/tech/assemblyai/assemblyai-llm-gateway)
An OpenAI-compatible API for calling frontier language models like Claude, GPT, and Gemini inside voice AI workflows, replacing the deprecated LeMUR product.

## Key Features

**99-language transcription with native code-switching**
Universal-3.5 Pro transcribes 99 languages and natively handles audio that switches between 18 of them mid-conversation, without requiring a language to be selected in advance.

**Fully managed voice agent pipeline**
The Voice Agent API bundles speech-to-text, LLM reasoning, text-to-speech, turn detection, and tool calling into one WebSocket connection, with native plugins for LiveKit and Pipecat.

**Modular audio intelligence**
Sentiment analysis, entity detection, topic detection, PII redaction, and content moderation are available as add-ons on top of any transcription request, priced and billed independently.

## Use Cases

**Voice agents and conversational AI**
Build speech-to-speech agents with the Voice Agent API, deployed through LiveKit, Pipecat, Twilio, or Telnyx telephony.

**Contact center and sales call analytics**
Combine transcription with sentiment analysis, topic detection, and entity detection to analyze calls at scale.

**Meeting intelligence and medical documentation**
Use contextual prompting and Speech Understanding add-ons to generate accurate transcripts and summaries for meetings and clinical audio.
