# Vibe Generation

Vibe Generation is a project founded by Paolo Bonetti to integrate specialized professionals and AI tools into the real-world processes of businesses. It describes itself as a manifesto more than a brand, rooted in observing how AI tools change people's roles in production processes. The model supports companies, brands, and organizations in moving from occasional AI tool use to hybrid teams and processes, where technology and human skills work together.

| General | |
|---------|--|
| **Project** | Vibe Generation |
| **Founder** | Paolo Bonetti, a Milan-based digital entrepreneur and engineer who also founded Hybrid Digital Consultancy |
| **Type** | AI adoption and organizational transformation |

---

## Focus Areas

**Hybrid teams and processes**
Helping organizations combine AI tools with specialized professionals, so that technology and human skills work in the same process.

**Talent and judgment**
Treating taste, interpretation, and judgment as central skills as AI tools take over routine production work, an idea closely tied to vibe coding, where people describe what they want and AI builds it.

**Organizational transformation**
Looking at how AI changes roles, skills, and the structure of companies, not only which tools they use.

---

## Who It Is For

Bonetti has said the project is aimed at everyone, not only younger people. It is relevant to companies, brands, and organizations moving from experimenting with AI tools to building lasting hybrid ways of working.
